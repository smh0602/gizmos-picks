How to read the badges. Model is ours — fitted, backtested, and carrying a confidence number. Market is the sportsbooks' opinion, de-vigged. It is not our projection. Live is plain fact from the MLB feed.

We model two markets: starting-pitcher strikeouts and outs. Everything else is market data or unbuilt, and is labelled as such.

About the odds. Every price here is exactly what the sportsbook feed returned, stamped with the minute it was pulled — and lines move constantly, so a price is only ever right as of that minute. Check the age shown beside it and confirm at the book before placing anything. Where a fair probability appears it is de-vigged inside each book and then averaged across books; a one-sided quote gets no fair price at all rather than a guessed one.
